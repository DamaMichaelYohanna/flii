# Flii - AI Safety & Grounding Rules

To ensure maximum credibility, accuracy, and applicant trust, all AI operations within Flii MUST adhere to the following non-negotiable principles:

## 1. Zero Hallucination of Applicant Facts
- The AI **must never** invent achievements, employers, grades, GPAs, test scores, awards, publications, experiences, or project outcomes.
- Any application response generated by the AI must strictly reference facts documented in the user's `ApplicantProfile` or `StoryBank`.
- If required information is missing to answer an essay prompt, the AI must explicitly flag:
  > *"I need additional information from you regarding [Missing Context]."*
- When drafting content, the AI must highlight any claims that require user verification or evidence upload.

## 2. Zero Hallucination of Opportunity Criteria
- The AI **must never** invent scholarship eligibility rules, application deadlines, award stipends, or document requirements.
- All opportunity requirements must be linked to a verified source URL and timestamp.
- If published requirements are ambiguous or incomplete, eligibility status must be marked as `needs_clarification`, not `confirmed_eligible`.

## 3. Responsible Fit Language
- **Do not** display arbitrary probabilities of admission (e.g. *"94% chance of winning"*).
- Use **Profile Fit** and provide transparent requirement-by-requirement explanations (e.g. *"Nationality requirement satisfied"*, *"Degree classification matches 2:1/First Class threshold"*).
- Never claim admission or scholarship award is guaranteed.

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop Approval Required
- The AI is prohibited from autonomously executing consequential actions.
- Any action involving:
  - Submitting an application
  - Sending emails or recommendation requests to referees
  - Modifying or deleting official documents
  - Making legal or factual declarations
  **MUST** generate an `AgentAction` in `PENDING` state and require explicit human approval (`APPROVED` / `REJECTED`).

## 5. Structured Data Integrity
- All AI model responses must be validated against structured Pydantic schemas before database persistence.
- If parsing fails, the system logs the incident, falls back gracefully, and notifies the user without corrupting workspace state.
