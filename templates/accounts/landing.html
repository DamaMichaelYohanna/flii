Frequently Asked Questions

Who is ScholarFlow designed for?

ScholarFlow is built for ambitious graduates—with initial focus on Nigerian and African scholars—applying for fully-funded Master's programmes, Erasmus Mundus, DAAD, Chevening, and global fellowships.

How does ScholarFlow prevent AI hallucinations?

ScholarFlow builds a Personal Knowledge Base from your verified profile, transcripts, and Story Bank. The AI is structurally constrained to only reference documented facts and will flag any missing context.

Does ScholarFlow guarantee scholarship selection?

No. ScholarFlow provides realistic Profile Fit analysis and high-quality preparation tools based on published criteria. Scholarship selection is decided solely by official award committees.
